Since 2024, MULTIPLAN AGRO CULTURE FARMS LTD has been empowering farmers, improving food security, and delivering innovative agricultural solutions that create lasting impact.

Who We Are

MULTIPLAN AGRO CULTURE FARMS LTD is a Nigerian agribusiness company established in 2024 with a commitment to promoting sustainable agriculture, food security, and economic growth. We provide innovative agricultural solutions that improve productivity while preserving natural resources for future generations.

Through farm mechanization, crop production, livestock farming, aquatic production, agricultural consultancy, and farmer support services, we help individuals, communities, and organizations achieve sustainable agricultural success.

Our Vision

To pioneer sustainable agricultural solutions that ensure environmental preservation, economic growth, and food security for present and future generations.

Our Mission

  • To foster sustainable farming practices that improve productivity while safeguarding natural resources.
  • To provide financial, technological, and educational support to farmers.
  • To innovate solutions for climate resilience and global agricultural challenges.
